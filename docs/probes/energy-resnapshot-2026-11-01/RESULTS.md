> (0.095 Wh per case-run).** Tag: `metered: powermetrics package power (CPU+GPU+ANE), laptop (MacBookPro18,1 · M1 Pro), serial`.

**Not evidence:**
- Anything after ≈18:50 on 1 Nov: coding agents shared the laptop with the GPU jobs (K4 ASR, L1), so those windows are upper bounds and are not reported here.
- Wall-socket energy: package power excludes the display, DRAM, SSD and charger losses. The figures are a floor on what the wall meter would show.
- A comparison with hosted models. No provider publishes per-request energy for OUR tasks or for API calls. The figures that do exist are for consumer prompts, measured differently: Google gives a median Gemini Apps text prompt of 0.24 Wh (full stack: accelerators, host CPU and DRAM, idle capacity and data-centre overhead; 0.10 Wh for accelerators alone; arXiv 2508.15734, Aug 2025). OpenAI's only figure is about 0.34 Wh per average ChatGPT query, from a 2025 blog line with no method. Mistral publishes carbon and water, not Wh. Against our package-power floor, any "x× less than the cloud" line would be modelled across mismatched scopes, never metered.
- The idle subtraction: at 72 mW against about 24–30 W it removes about 0.3%. The idle window was quieter than an attended laptop, so the gross figure is the conservative one to quote.

## Reading
- **Repeatable:** load-to-load spread is ≤0.002 Wh per case on every exam, and coverage is ≥0.994 of each window.
- **Model size, not exam, drives the per-token cost.**
  - The 2B champions run at about 30 W and use about 400–535 J per 1k output tokens.
  - The 4B champions run at about 24 W and use about 625–750 J per 1k output tokens. They draw less power but take longer per token.
  - Long-input exams (transcript-en, recap, call-fields) cost the most per case: 0.13–0.17 Wh.
- **For scale:** at 0.095 Wh per case-run, 10,000 tasks of this mix is about 0.95 kWh of package energy (`modelled on` this run's mix; a client's mix will differ).
- **Next step for the energy column:** this is the first metered Wh-per-task figure for the score table (lead 19 Oct). Repeat the capture on a clean window for any new champion.
